Effects

Hover an effect to play its preview, click it to apply it (Undo brings yours back). ▾ shows its ranges and tags, and ★ marks the default that Reset all restores.

Spectrum

Drag a box's sides to set its frequency range. The solid line is the threshold where the segment starts to light, the dashed line is full brightness. Drag empty space to add a segment.

LED strip

Live output. Add lows, mids or highs to the strip, then drag segments sideways to move them, up or down to another row, their ends to resize, or drag on empty space to create one. Overlapping segments mix, brightest wins.

Segments

Colors by range

Recolor every segment in a part of the spectrum at once. A segment belongs to the range its frequency band is centered in.

Reactive presets

How a segment reacts: frequency range, threshold and motion. Colors are not changed.

Selected segment

Ambient light

What the strip shows when audio reactive is off, or in Auto once the music has been quiet for a while. It fades in and out.
